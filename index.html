From discovery to a considered order

How to use a CSSBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet organizes research: product links, quoted prices and selected options. It does not replace a seller listing, an agent’s checkout or an item-specific inspection.

01 / FIND THE SOURCE

Identify the exact item

Open the CAFANS product page, then locate the original seller URL where available. Save the item ID, seller, chosen color and size. A catalog homepage is not an orderable marketplace listing.

02 / VERIFY THE OPTIONS

Check before ordering

If using CSSBuy, enter the original seller link in your account and confirm it resolves to the intended item. If it fails, ask the agent for guidance. Check current charges and seller terms before paying.

03 / REVIEW THE PARCEL

Inspect, then arrange delivery

Review available warehouse photos against the ordered variation. Resolve missing measurements or visible issues, then compare eligible delivery routes, packing choices and the final shipping quote.

Give every row a purpose

Compare more than the thumbnail

  • Shoes: record the stated sizing system, width and measurement method. Foot length and insole length are different measurements.
  • Hoodies and T-shirts: compare chest width, shoulders and length with a garment that fits you. Note whether dimensions are flat or circumference.
  • Jackets: allow for your intended layers and compare sleeve measurements using the same reference points.
  • QC photos: check the selected color, quantity, visible condition and requested measurements. Photos do not establish authenticity or long-term durability.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize finds before ordering through a buying agent. Useful entries include the original seller link, selected variation, quoted price, measurement notes and the date checked. This page provides CAFANS catalog examples and a CSV shortlist tool.

Where do the CSSBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both open https://cafans.com. Individual product cards open their corresponding CAFANS detail pages. Neither main button completes a purchase or opens a CSSBuy checkout.

Are these live prices and confirmed available products?

No. The cards show a snapshot sourced on November 19, 2026. USD amounts are indicative catalog prices. Open the current listing to confirm price, availability, quantity and selected options.

Can every product be purchased through CSSBuy?

Compatibility is not confirmed here. Locate the original seller URL and check whether CSSBuy can load or handle that exact listing. Confirm current purchasing and shipping restrictions with the agent before paying.

Does the listed price include international delivery?

No. Budget separately for domestic delivery, applicable service and payment fees, international freight, optional services and any destination charges. Use current quotes and keep unknown amounts visible.

Are the product images warehouse QC photos?

No. These images come from the CAFANS catalog. For your own order, inspect the warehouse photographs available in your agent account and request specific measurements or close-ups when needed.

Can I download a spreadsheet?

Yes. With JavaScript enabled, use Save on the products you want, then select Export shortlist CSV. The download contains names, categories, indicative prices, CAFANS URLs and blank fields for your own seller links and notes.

Is this the official CSSBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery page pointing to CAFANS. CSSBuy and the product names shown belong to their respective owners. Orders, payments and support are handled by the relevant external provider.
